# 政策页文案(粘贴到 Settings → Policies)

## Refund policy

**Services (Mini Diagnostic, Audit Sprint).** Full refund if you cancel before we receive your data export. Once analysis has begun, refunds are pro-rated at our discretion. If we fail to deliver within the stated window, you receive a full refund automatically.

**Physical pre-orders.** Cancel any time before your order ships for an immediate full refund. If we anticipate missing the stated ship-by date, we will notify you by email and offer a full refund or the option to wait — your choice (per the FTC Mail Order Rule). After delivery: 30-day returns, unworn/unused, buyer pays return shipping; refund issued within 5 business days of receiving the return.

## Shipping policy

Physical pre-order items ship by the date stated on the product page at the time of your order. You'll receive tracking by email the day your order ships. Current shipping scope: United States (lower 48). Digital/service products: nothing ships — delivery is by email per the product description.

> **Service data.** Conversation logs you share for diagnostics are used solely to deliver your engagement, are never used to train models, and are deleted 30 days after delivery unless you request earlier deletion.
